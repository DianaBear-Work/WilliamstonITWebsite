Book time with the onsite IT Analyst for support that benefits from a scheduled conversation. An existing IT ticket is required for every appointment.

HOW IT WORKS

Choose a time that works for you.

Before scheduling an appointment, submit an IT ticket through ServiceNow and make note of your ticket number. You must include that ticket number when booking your appointment.

IT Ticket Required for All Appointments

Appointments booked without a valid IT ticket number will be canceled.

The ticket allows IT to document your issue, review the details, and track the work needed before and after your appointment.

1. Submit an IT ticket through ServiceNow.

2. Save your ticket number.

3. Schedule your appointment and include the ticket number and a brief description of the issue.

4. Keep your ticket number available during the appointment.
