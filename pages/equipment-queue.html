Equipment Request Process

  1. Review the quantities listed in the inventory.
  2. Select the appropriate laptop or accessories request link.
  3. Complete and submit the official ServiceNow request.
  4. IT reviews the request and coordinates fulfillment through the established process.

The official ServiceNow request is the record of your submission. The inventory page itself does not collect or store requests.
