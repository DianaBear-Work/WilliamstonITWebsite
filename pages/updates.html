Projects in Progress

Project status and the latest progress at Pelzer 2.

These initiatives focus on improving access to engineering information, supporting production and office operations, and creating better ways to communicate IT information across the site.

In Progress

Stores Kiosk for Quality Engineers

Developing a dedicated kiosk in the Stores area so authorized users can look up and view engineering drawings without relying on another department to retrieve them.

The kiosk is planned to be locked to Oracle Fusion Product Development to provide access to the intended drawing resources.

Focus: Restricted kiosk access and engineering drawing availability.

Location Assessment

Smart Run Printer Relocation

Worked with Encore to assess whether Ethernet drops could be installed in the Smart Run desk area for the Smart Run printer.

The area is too far from the MDF and IDF for the proposed Ethernet installation, so the the Smart Run Printer will need to move.

Finding: The proposed network-drop location needs an alternative solution.

In Progress

Supervisor Desk Relocation

Coordinating the network requirements for the supervisor desk relocation, including working to have Ethernet drops installed in the new desk area.

Next step: Coordinate installation and confirm connectivity requirements.

Awaiting Equipment

Production Floor Kiosk

The kiosk build is complete. The remaining items are the correct cable and desktop mount needed to finish the physical setup.

Next step: Complete installation once the required cable and mount arrive.

In Progress

Office Display for Tier 2 IT Presentations

Preparing a display in the new office area to support monthly IT updates during Tier 2 meetings.

The planned sessions will include site IT updates, demonstrations of tools and processes, and helpful reminders for managers.

Goal: Establish a recurring, practical IT update and demonstration session.

Pending Approval

Pelzer 2 IT Website

Developing a simple website for Pelzer 2 employees to find IT resources, troubleshooting guidance, updates, and appointment information in one place.

The proposed site would be accessible through a QR code, making it easier for employees to reach support information.

Important: The website still requires full approval before implementation or rollout to employees.

Project status: Statuses reflect current progress and may change as equipment, installation schedules, technical requirements, and approvals are confirmed.
